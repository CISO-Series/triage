    HOW TO PLAY
    
  
    Each round shows two recent CVEs with their CVSS scores hidden. A higher CVSS score means a more severe vulnerability: more risk if it's exploited. Tap the one you think is more severe. Both scores are then revealed, right or wrong, and you move on.
    
  
    There are 10 rounds. Your final score is how many of the 10 more severe vulnerabilities you correctly picked, like 7/10.
    
  
    The pairs get progressively harder as you advance: the two scores get closer together, so the call gets tougher.
    
  
    
  
    Everyone gets the same pairs each week. They rotate every Friday at 12:00 AM PT. Your first run counts; after that, practice as much as you like.
    
  
    Keys: ← left card, → right card. 🔥 means the CVE is on CISA's Known Exploited list.
